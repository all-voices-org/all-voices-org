## Welcome to the All Voices Educator Community!

## Understanding English Language Learners

### What is an ELL?

English Language Learner (ELL) is an abbreviation for adults who are actively learning the English language. This could be for various reasons, such as improving job prospects, seeking access to educational opportunities, or enhancing social and cultural interactions in an English-speaking environment.

**Important:** ELLs are not a monolith! Each learner brings unique experiences, backgrounds, and goals to their language learning journey.

### Other Terms to Know

- **ESL Students** (English as a Second Language) - Used in educational settings for students learning English in addition to their native language
- **LEP Students** (Limited English Proficient) - Used in legal and policy contexts
- **ELs** (English Learners) - A more inclusive contemporary term that acknowledges the learning process without implying a deficit
- **Multilingual Learners** - Emphasizes linguistic diversity and multilingual abilities
- **Emergent Bilingual** - Highlights developing bilingualism and values native language as an asset
