## Our Mission

All Voices aims to eliminate language as a barrier to success by educating local English language learners (ELLs) to bolster their communication skills, build confidence, and enable them to succeed in Johnson County and beyond. We believe in using a multifaceted approach to obtain this goal:

- **Empowering educators** by providing comprehensive training and teaching resources to existing and future volunteer English teachers
- **Building connections** between volunteer English educators and ELLs as conversation partners
- **Increasing access** to English education for local ELLs
- **Strengthening community relationships** and fostering mutual understanding

## Our Story

For the past two years, Josie has been dedicated to teaching English to immigrant and refugee adults in Johnson County, collaborating with other volunteers and witnessing the pressing need for resources and comprehensive English teacher training. In the summer of 2024, she became a Gilman Alumni Ambassador and traveled to Washington, D.C. for a four-day training session, where she learned about the Gilman Alumni Changemaker Grant.

She immediately thought of the English language learner community in Johnson County and reached out to Olga, as she shares Josie's belief that English should never be a barrier to anyone's success. Together, they developed the project All Voices and applied for the grant.

## What We Offer

Through collaboration, education, and compassion, we work to strengthen confidence, foster mutual understanding, and empower individuals to achieve success. Together, we foster understanding, celebrate cultural diversity, and create opportunities for growth and connection.

**All Voices resources are always free to use!**

All resources are available in multiple languages: English, Spanish, French, Arabic, Kiswahili, and Haitian Creole.
