## What We Offer

All Voices is dedicated to supporting adult English language learners (ELLs) as they build the confidence and communication skills needed to thrive. We connect learners in Johnson County with motivated English educators, foster understanding, celebrate cultural diversity, and create opportunities for growth and connection.

### Available Languages

Our resources and support are available in multiple languages to better serve our diverse community:

- **English**
- **Español** ([Spanish](/es))
- **Français** ([French](/fr))
- **العربية** ([Arabic](/ar))
- **Kiswahili** ([Swahili](/sw))
- **Kreyòl Ayisyen** ([Haitian Creole](/ht))

### Resource Topics

We offer comprehensive resources covering essential life skills and situations that English language learners encounter in Johnson County:

- **Johnson County Culture** - Learn about local culture and customs
- **Bussing & Transportation** - Navigate public transportation and getting around
- **Shopping** - Essential shopping skills and vocabulary
- **Health Care** - Healthcare system navigation and medical communication
- **Parent Responsibilities** - Understanding parental duties and school systems
- **Adult Responsibilities** - Civic duties and community participation
- **Work Situations** - Workplace communication and professional skills
- **Ordering Food** - Restaurant and food service interactions
- **Talking on the Phone** - Phone communication skills and etiquette
- **Interviewing** - Job interview preparation and skills
- **Meeting New People & Social Skills** - Building relationships and social interactions

Our resources come in various formats to accommodate different learning styles and situations. You'll find **instructional videos** for visual and auditory learners, **PDF documents** that you can download and print for offline use, **text-based guides** for quick reference, and **links to external resources** that connect you to important community services and additional learning materials. This variety ensures that whether you prefer to watch, read, or interact with content online, you'll find resources that work best for your learning needs.

---

**All Voices resources are always free to use!**

_All Voices aims to eliminate language as a barrier to success by educating local English language learners to bolster their communication skills, build confidence, and enable them to succeed in Johnson County and beyond._
